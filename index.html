Why Builders & Homeowners Love the Novaro T3

No complicated jargon—just four practical reasons why the Novaro T3 adds instant value to any new build or bathroom renovation.

01

Saves Space & Cleans Easily

Because the bowl floats off the floor and measures just 580mm deep, bathrooms feel noticeably bigger. With no base touching the tiles, mops and robot vacuums glide right underneath with zero dust traps.

02

All-in-One 400kg Steel Frame

No need to source a separate cistern. Every unit uses a rust-resistant, concealed steel water tank frame tested to hold 400kg—with High and Low tank options to fit standard stud walls or false walls.

03

100% Hands-Free Hygiene

Walk up and the microwave radar sensor lifts the lid automatically. Need to lift the seat or flush? Just tap the base sensor with your foot. Walk away and it closes and flushes on its own.

04

Instant Warmth & UV Self-Cleaning

Heats washing water instantly (no cold surprises), warms the seat across 4 comfort levels, dries with warm air, and sterilizes its ceramic nozzle with UV light after every use.
