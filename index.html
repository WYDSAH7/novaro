The Novaro Advantage

Why Specify the Novaro T3?

Engineered from the inside out to solve spatial, structural, and sanitary challenges in modern Australian bathrooms.

Space-Saving Floating Form

Measuring a compact 580 × 400 × 420mm, the wall-hung profile maximizes floor space while maintaining a full-sized, ergonomic 300 × 220mm comfortable seating area. Elevated off the tiles, it leaves zero dead corners—allowing mops and robot vacuums to glide effortlessly underneath.

400kg Concealed Steel Frame

Supported by a heavy-duty, anti-rust and anti-corrosion concealed cistern bracket tested to bear up to 400kg. Available in high or low in-wall tank configurations to suit both cavity walls and false walls without requiring a separate cistern purchase.

Touchless Radar & Foot Control

Equipped with microwave radar wave sensors that automatically open the lid upon approach and close/flush upon departure. Integrated capacitive foot-touch sensors allow hands-free lid opening, seat flipping, and flushing.

Clinical UV & Instant Heating

Features ultraviolet (UV) ceramic nozzle sterilization, double water filtration, an anti-fouling glaze, and an anti-bacterial flame-retardant seat. Enjoy instant water heating, warm air drying, and reliable mechanical flushing even during power outages.
